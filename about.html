About AI Pulse

Daily AI news, the numbers behind it, and what to do next.

AI Pulse is a daily brief on the AI developments that matter to business leaders – in procurement, finance, technology, pharma, policy and beyond. Every story is written in plain language, with the key figures and specific next steps for the people who will need to act on it.

What each brief covers

One issue a day, checked for new stories every two hours

Each issue covers a stated time window and holds up to eight stories, chosen for their significance to executives. Every story follows the same structure:

  • What happened – a plain-language account of the development.
  • Why it matters – the business context in a few sentences.
  • Key numbers – the figures that matter, exactly as the source reports them.
  • Sector impact and what to actually do – a specific action and timeframe for named roles, such as CIOs, CFOs, procurement and compliance leads.
  • Source – a link to the original report or announcement, with its date.
How each brief is made

Every fact comes from a source you can check

  • Every story, figure, name and date is checked against the original source page – a company announcement, regulatory filing, government site or established news outlet – and that source is linked on the page.
  • Figures are given exactly as the source states them, including qualifiers such as "about" or "up to". Any number worked out from sourced figures is labeled as calculated.
  • Claims are attributed to whoever made them. Stories that can't be verified, rumors without a named outlet, and stories where AI isn't central are left out.
  • Stories are written in our own words, with at most one short, attributed quote per source. The analysis and recommendations are our own.
  • AI Pulse uses AI tools to help find, research and draft stories, working under these rules.
Corrections

Errors are fixed on the page

If a published story turns out to be wrong, it is corrected on the page from the original source.

Reading AI Pulse

Today's brief, every past issue, and a link for each story

Today's brief is always on the home page. Every issue also has a permanent page in the archive, and each story on it has its own link, so you can share or cite exactly the story you mean. On any issue page you can ask questions about that day's stories with the Ask AI Pulse assistant.
